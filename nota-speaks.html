01 · Client background

Where the story starts.

NOTA Speaks is an independent, citizen-led platform focused on civic education and democratic participation in India.

The organisation was founded with a clear mission: An informed citizen is the king of democracy. NOTA Speaks creates content and resources around legal awareness, constitutional literacy, and the rights and responsibilities of Indian citizens.

The platform needed a digital home that matched the seriousness and clarity of its mission — a place where citizens could learn, engage, and understand their role in a democracy.

03 · Project objectives

What we set out to achieve.

Each objective is paired with the design or development decision it shaped.

  1. 1

    Create a professional digital home for civic education

    Designed a clean, editorial layout inspired by trusted informational platforms — avoiding social media clutter and noise.

  2. 2

    Make legal knowledge accessible to ordinary citizens

    Built a structured knowledge hub with categories, clear navigation, and plain-language content presentation.

  3. 3

    Present the organisation's team and campaigns credibly

    Created dedicated team and campaign sections with editorial photography and structured biography formats.

  4. 4

    Build an administrative infrastructure for content management

    Developed an administrative dashboard as part of the project scope. The current public-facing website is informational.

04 · Design direction

Serious, clear and human.

The visual language of NOTA Speaks needed to feel credible and trustworthy without being cold or bureaucratic.

01

Editorial typography

Display serif for headings combined with a clean sans-serif body font — borrowed from quality journalism.

02

Restrained colour

A disciplined palette: deep navy or dark teal for authority, a warm accent for calls to action, ample white space.

03

Content-first layout

No decorative noise. Every layout decision serves the reading experience — hierarchy, flow and clarity above all.

04

Accessible by default

WCAG-compliant contrast, semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, screen reader support built in from the start.

06 · Development process

Built to scale with the mission.

01

Discovery & planning

Deep-dive into the organisation's mission, content strategy, and target audiences. Defined site architecture and content hierarchy.

02

Design system

Created a typography system, colour palette, and component library. Prototype tested with sample content before development began.

03

Frontend development

Built with React (Vite) for performance and maintainability. Responsive layouts with smooth scroll interactions and micro-animations.

04

Administrative dashboard

Developed a backend administrative interface as part of the project scope to support content management and organisational operations. The current public-facing website is informational and does not represent live operational status of all backend features.

05

Launch & SEO

Full meta implementation, structured data (Schema.org), image optimisation and accessibility audit before launch.

08 · Technology stack

Built on solid foundations.

Technology choices were made for performance, maintainability, and the organisation's long-term ability to manage content independently.

  • React (Vite)
  • JavaScript
  • CSS / Vanilla CSS
  • Schema.org structured data
  • Google Fonts
  • Responsive images
  • Open Graph metadata
  • WCAG accessibility

React + Vite

Component-based architecture for reusability and performance. Vite provides fast build times and excellent developer experience.

SEO & structured data

Full Schema.org implementation, Open Graph tags, and canonical URLs ensure the platform is discoverable by the audiences it serves.

Performance

Image optimisation, lazy loading, and minimal third-party dependencies keep page load times fast even on lower-end devices.
