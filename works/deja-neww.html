Deja-Neww is a lifestyle and fashion platform with a contemporary identity — designed to serve a style-conscious audience looking for curated fashion discovery and brand engagement. The name itself reflects the platform's personality: familiar yet refreshingly new.

The platform called for a digital experience that matched its aesthetic sensibility — something that felt premium, editorial and visually distinctive without being cold or inaccessible.

This project was led by Divyanshi as part of her selected work at OmniOps Digital — bringing her design sensibility and attention to brand voice directly to the Deja-Neww experience.

03

The challenge

What we needed to solve

1

Distinctive brand identity

In the lifestyle and fashion space, visual identity is everything. The platform needed a digital presence that felt genuinely distinctive — not generic or interchangeable with competitors.

2

Audience connection

Style-conscious audiences are discerning and quickly form strong impressions of a brand's credibility. The experience needed to immediately communicate taste and curatorial quality.

3

Discovery experience

A fashion platform lives or dies on its ability to inspire discovery — presenting the brand's offerings in a way that invites exploration rather than just browsing.

4

Visual performance

Fashion platforms are image-heavy. The website needed to balance strong visual presentation with acceptable performance — ensuring images loaded quickly without compromising quality.

04

Design direction

Editorial, curated, contemporary.

1

Editorial visual language

An editorial aesthetic that treats product and lifestyle imagery with the same care as a fashion publication — using generous whitespace, confident typography and restrained colour to let the content breathe.

2

Brand personality in motion

Subtle hover animations, scroll reveals and interaction details that reflect the platform's contemporary personality — making the digital experience feel alive and considered rather than static.

3

Curated discovery layout

Layout choices that encourage exploration — grid arrangements, visual hierarchy and navigation structures designed to guide users deeper into the platform rather than leading them to exit.

4

Mobile-first fashion experience

Fashion audiences are overwhelmingly mobile. The entire experience was designed with touch interaction and mobile viewport in mind first — with desktop refinements built from that foundation.

06

Development approach

How we built it

1

Brand and audience discovery

We began with a thorough understanding of Deja-Neww's brand identity, audience profile and aspirations — to ensure the digital experience genuinely reflected the platform's character.

2

Visual design and editorial direction

An editorial visual direction was developed — with careful attention to typography, colour palette, imagery treatment and layout choices that set the brand apart in the lifestyle space.

3

Interaction design

Hover states, scroll animations and navigation transitions were designed to add personality and quality feel to the experience — reflecting the brand's contemporary sensibility.

4

Mobile-first build and testing

The site was built mobile-first, with particular attention to image performance, touch interaction and the discovery experience on smaller screens.

5

Deployment on Render

The platform was deployed on Render with appropriate configuration for the application's requirements, ensuring reliable availability and acceptable load times.
