REEVA AQUATECH is a manufacturer and supplier of premium water storage solutions — tanks, containers and related aquatech products. In a category where product quality and reliability are paramount, the brand needed a website that communicated those values from the first impression.

The water storage market serves both domestic and commercial customers. The website needed to present the product range clearly — with accurate descriptions and quality imagery — to support purchase decisions at every level.

A professional brand presentation was essential to differentiate REEVA from commodity competitors and position the brand as a trusted, quality-focused manufacturer.

03

The challenge

What we needed to solve

1

Premium brand positioning in a commodity market

Water storage is often perceived as a commodity category. The design needed to elevate REEVA's brand — making the website feel premium and trustworthy without appearing overpriced or inaccessible.

2

Clear product presentation

The product range needed organised, clear presentation — with distinct images and descriptions for each product category. Repetitive or unclear product display would undermine confidence in the brand.

3

Business information and contact

Both domestic customers and commercial buyers needed accessible contact and business information — to make enquiries, request specifications or understand the company's scale and credibility.

4

Responsive performance

Customers in the manufacturing and distribution sector access supplier websites across devices. The website needed to perform well on mobile — where many purchase enquiries are initiated.

04

Design direction

Clean, credible, premium.

1

Quality-first visual identity

A clean, professional visual identity that projects product quality and manufacturing credibility — using considered typography, an assured colour palette and product photography treated with care.

2

Distinct product imagery

Each product category uses a distinct image — avoiding the repetition that undermines product presentation. Unique imagery for each product reinforces the breadth of REEVA's range.

3

Information clarity

Product descriptions are concise and accurate — giving customers the information they need to make informed decisions without unnecessary complexity or technical jargon.

4

Navigation simplicity

Clear, straightforward navigation from homepage to product categories and contact — ensuring domestic and commercial customers can find what they need without friction.

05

Key features

What the website delivers

Homepage with brand introduction and product overview

Contact section for product enquiries and business information

Navigation designed for quick product discovery

Professional brand identity applied throughout

Fully responsive across desktop, tablet and mobile

06

Development approach

How we built it

1

Product catalogue mapping

We began by mapping the complete product range — identifying categories, understanding product specifications and establishing a clear structure for presenting the range in a logical, navigable way.

2

Brand and visual direction

A premium visual direction was developed — clean, assured and quality-forward — to differentiate REEVA from commodity competitors and position the brand as a trusted manufacturer.

3

Product page development

Each product category was developed with distinct imagery and clear, accurate descriptions. A consistent layout was used across all product pages to create a coherent catalogue experience.

4

Responsive build and testing

The site was tested across desktop, tablet and mobile — with particular attention to product image display and contact section usability on smaller screens.

5

Vercel deployment

The website was deployed on Vercel for reliable performance and fast loading times — appropriate for a product website where first impressions of quality matter.
