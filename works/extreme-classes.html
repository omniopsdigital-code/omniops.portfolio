Extreme Classes is a coaching institute focused on preparing students for some of India's most competitive examinations — IIT JEE, NEET and Class 11 and 12 board examinations. The institute offers structured academic programmes delivered by an experienced faculty team.

A key part of the institute's offering is War of Minds, a learning platform that forms an integral part of the student experience — providing study materials, test series and competitive preparation resources.

The website needed to present the institute's programmes, faculty, study resources and student enquiry pathways in a way that inspired confidence in students and their families at a critical decision-making stage.

03

The challenge

What we needed to solve

1

Complex programme portfolio

Extreme Classes offers multiple programmes — IIT JEE, NEET, board exam preparation, Class 11 and 12 courses — each with distinct audiences and requirements. The website needed to present each clearly without overwhelming visitors.

2

Faculty credibility

Students and parents rely heavily on faculty quality when choosing a coaching institute. The design needed to present the teaching team with appropriate authority and warmth.

3

War of Minds platform introduction

The institute's War of Minds learning platform needed clear, prominent presentation — communicating its purpose and value to prospective students without overpromising on functionality.

4

Student enquiry conversion

The website needed clear, accessible pathways for students and parents to make enquiries, with contact information prominently placed throughout the user journey.

5

Results and social proof

The institute's student results and outcomes needed to be presented in a way that built trust — accurately, without fabrication or exaggeration.

04

Project objectives

What we set out to achieve

  • 1Create a structured, comprehensive website that clearly presents all coaching programmes for Class 11, 12, IIT JEE and NEET.
  • 2Design an authoritative faculty section that builds confidence in the institute's teaching quality and expertise.
  • 3Present the War of Minds learning platform with clarity — explaining its purpose and value within the student experience.
  • 4Build clear, prominent enquiry pathways to reduce friction for students and parents at the decision-making stage.
  • 5Present student results and study materials sections that reflect the institute's academic credibility.
  • 6Ensure the website performs well on mobile, where the majority of prospective students are likely to first encounter the institute.
05

Design direction

Serious, approachable, aspirational.

1

Academic authority

The design communicates seriousness and credibility — appropriate for families making important educational investment decisions — while remaining approachable and not intimidating for students.

2

Programme clarity

Each coaching programme — IIT JEE, NEET, board exam preparation — has clear, dedicated presentation. Visitors can quickly identify the programme relevant to them without navigation confusion.

3

Aspirational student imagery

Visual language that reflects student ambition and academic aspiration — aligned with the competitive examination context without being overwhelming or pressurising.

4

Mobile-first layout

Recognising that students predominantly use mobile devices, every section was designed and tested for mobile performance first, with desktop experience built from that foundation.

06

Key features

What the website delivers

Coaching Programmes

Dedicated pages for each programme — IIT JEE preparation, NEET preparation, Class 11 and 12 academic coaching, and board examination preparation. Each programme section presents curriculum structure, preparation approach and study methodology.

  • IIT JEE preparation programme
  • NEET preparation programme
  • Class 11 and 12 subject coaching
  • Board examination preparation
  • Integrated course pathways

Faculty Profiles

A dedicated faculty section presents the teaching team with profiles designed to communicate expertise, subject specialisation and teaching experience. Faculty presentation is a primary trust signal for prospective students and parents.

  • Individual faculty profiles
  • Subject and specialisation display
  • Teaching background presentation
  • Approachable visual treatment

War of Minds Platform

The War of Minds learning platform is presented as a key differentiator of the Extreme Classes experience. The website section explains the platform's purpose, the types of resources available — including test series and study materials — and how it supports student preparation.

  • Platform overview and purpose
  • Test series presentation
  • Study materials section
  • Platform access information

Student Resources

The website includes sections for study materials and test series — presenting what is available to students as part of their coaching programme. Results sections display student achievements to support the institute's credibility.

  • Study materials overview
  • Test series information
  • Student results section
  • Downloadable resource links where available

Enquiry Experience

Multiple contact touchpoints throughout the website ensure that students and parents can reach the institute without friction. The enquiry experience is designed to reduce barriers at the decision-making moment.

  • Contact form for programme enquiries
  • Phone and email prominently displayed
  • Location and directions information
  • WhatsApp enquiry pathway
07

Development approach

How we built it

1

Discovery & content mapping

We studied the institute's programme portfolio, understood the target student profiles and mapped out all the content sections needed — from homepage to individual programme pages, faculty profiles, resources and contact.

2

Information architecture

A clear site structure was designed to make navigation intuitive — students looking for IIT JEE information, NEET information or War of Minds resources could reach their content in two clicks or fewer.

3

Visual design

The visual direction was developed to feel aspirational and academically credible — using the institute's colour palette and typographic choices to create a consistent visual identity across all sections.

4

Responsive development

The site was developed mobile-first. Every section was tested across screen sizes, with particular attention to programme pages and the faculty section — the primary trust-building content for prospective students.

5

Content integration & launch

Faculty profiles, programme content, student results and War of Minds information were integrated, reviewed for accuracy and launched with the client's approval.
