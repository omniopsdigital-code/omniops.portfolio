01 · Client background

A modern multidisciplinary legal practice.

Sara Legal Solutions is a law firm in Ranchi, Jharkhand providing integrated legal, taxation, financial, regulatory, and business advisory services.

The firm's team includes Advocates enrolled with the Bar Council of Jharkhand and a Chartered Accountant — enabling a truly multidisciplinary approach to client matters. They practise before the Jharkhand High Court and district and subordinate courts across the state.

The firm's philosophy — "Listen Carefully. Prepare Thoroughly. Advocate Fearlessly." — guided every design and content decision we made for their website.

02 · Client requirements

Trust before anything else.

Legal websites carry an unusual burden: they must establish credibility before a single word is read.

01

Bar Council compliance

The website must comply with Bar Council of India rules — no solicitation, no advertising. A disclaimer overlay was required before entry.

02

Professional brand presentation

The firm needed to look as established and credible as it is — premium typography, measured layout, authoritative photography.

03

Clear practice area organisation

Ten practice areas — from civil litigation to GST compliance — needed to be presented clearly without overwhelming visitors.

04

Advocate profile pages

Individual profile pages for each team member with credentials, practice areas, and professional background.

05

Full mobile responsiveness

The majority of potential clients would visit on mobile devices. The website needed to work flawlessly at every screen size.

The disclaimer overlay

Indian law requires that advocate websites include a disclaimer that no solicitation is intended. We designed an elegant disclaimer overlay that respects this requirement while setting the right tone for the firm from the very first interaction.

The overlay includes the Bar Council of India text, a custom-styled checkbox, and clear action buttons — all in keeping with the firm's premium identity.

Gold as the accent

A warm gold (#B08D57) was chosen as the brand's accent colour — evoking scales of justice, authority, and longevity. Used sparingly: on rules, borders, the logo emblem, and key interactive elements.

The dark background (#0b0b0b) gives the website a premium, editorial quality — setting it apart from the generic blue-and-white legal websites common in the space.

04 · Design decisions

Why it looks and works this way.

A near-black background immediately signals that this is not a generic legal directory entry. It positions the firm as a premium, modern practice — while the gold accents maintain traditional legal authority.

Cormorant Garamond brings the weight and elegance of traditional legal typography — echoing the fonts found in law books and court documents — while remaining beautiful on screen at all sizes.

Ten practice areas could easily overwhelm. We presented them as numbered cards with SVG icons, short descriptions, and tagged sub-areas — making the breadth of the firm's practice clear without requiring the visitor to read everything.

Each advocate and CA has a dedicated profile page. The team cards on the main page use a clean hover-reveal interaction — "View Profile" — to invite exploration without crowding the layout.

Thin gold horizontal rules are used throughout as both section dividers and brand markers — referencing the measuring lines used in legal documents and adding visual structure without clutter.

05 · User experience

Designed for the visitor's first question.

Most visitors to a law firm's website arrive with one question: Can you help me with my problem?

The information architecture is built around that question. From the hero CTA ("Explore Practice Areas") to the clearly numbered practice area grid, every path leads the visitor toward understanding if this firm is the right fit.

The contact section is prominent and honest — modes of consultation are listed, office hours are clear, and an important disclaimer notes that initial contact does not create an attorney-client relationship.

07 · Development process

Clean code, clear structure.

01

Requirements & BCI compliance review

Reviewed Bar Council of India Rules on advocate advertising to ensure the website is fully compliant. The disclaimer overlay was the first component designed.

02

Design & typography system

Established the premium dark visual identity, colour palette, and typography system. Cormorant Garamond for display, Inter/Jost for body and UI elements.

03

Static HTML/CSS/JS development

Built with semantic HTML5, vanilla CSS, and vanilla JavaScript — ensuring maximum compatibility, performance, and ease of long-term maintenance.

04

Responsive optimisation

Full mobile, tablet, and desktop testing. Navigation collapses to a hamburger menu, cards reflow, and typography scales fluidly across all breakpoints.

05

SEO & structured data

Schema.org LegalService markup, Open Graph tags, canonical URLs, geo-location metadata (IN-JH), and Google Search Console verification.

08 · Technology stack

Simple, fast and maintainable.

Technology choices prioritised performance, compatibility, and the client's ability to maintain the website independently over time.

  • HTML5 (semantic)
  • Vanilla CSS
  • Vanilla JavaScript
  • Schema.org (LegalService)
  • Cormorant Garamond
  • Playfair Display
  • Inter / Jost
  • Open Graph metadata
  • Geo metadata (IN-JH)
  • Google Search Console

No framework required

A single-page website for a law firm doesn't need React or Vue. Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS means faster initial load, simpler hosting, and complete longevity.

LegalService schema

Schema.org's LegalService type enables rich search results — employee records, areaServed, openingHoursSpecification — improving local search visibility.

Photography treatment

Client-supplied photography is presented with CSS blend modes and overlay treatments that integrate naturally with the dark, premium visual identity.
